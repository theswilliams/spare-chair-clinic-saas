Scope: single landing page (Persuade). Audience: clinic owners and front-desk staff at small dental/physio clinics. Job: understand that a cancelled slot is refilled from the waitlist, then start a trial. Proof: a live, clickable plan of one clinic day (synthetic, labelled). Constraints: Astro + TypeScript, no UI framework, all numbers illustrative, testimonials fictional.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The diary is a box-office seating plan, a cancellation is a seat returned to sale, and the page refuses the SaaS default of dashboard screenshot plus three feature cards. The working demo is the hero, not a picture of one.
OWN-WORLD: Drenched ticket-blue field (cobalt), cream ticket stock for light sections, deep navy ink, one hot-pink signal used only for "returned / being offered". Seats are small back-rounded buttons with initials; free seats hollow, booked solid cream, returned pink. Bricolage Grotesque display at large scale, Source Serif 4 for running text, tabular figures for times. Perforations and stub edges as dividers. No cards, no shadows, no hairline tables.
STORY: A front-desk person sees a whole clinic day as seats, cancels one, watches the returns desk offer it down the waitlist in order (skipping people it cannot fit), and sees it resold. They believe the gap is handled, check the rough value in a calculator of their own numbers, and start a trial.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed cobalt. Headline "Cancellations go back on sale." left-aligned, very large, top-left; one-line sub and primary CTA beside/below it. Beneath: the wide day plan (4 chair rows by 12 half-hour columns), one seat already returned and pulsing pink, a status line under it. Fixed bottom ticket-bar nav with the trial stub at its right end.
FORM: Seating plan with returns queue, position 4 of 7 on the ordered list (the roll assigned index 4); seed key 3d174f85. Raise from a declined challenger (labanotation): time reads in one direction with the active beat banded across the plan.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
