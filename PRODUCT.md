# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro + TypeScript. Chosen by the user. Static output, no UI framework; small TypeScript modules for the interactive demo, pricing toggle and FAQ.

## Users

Primary: owners and practice managers of small dental and physiotherapy clinics (1 to 6 treatment rooms) who lose paid chair time to late cancellations and no-shows. Secondary: front-desk staff who phone round a waitlist by hand to refill a gap, usually while a patient stands at the counter. Buyers are time-poor, sceptical of software that promises "AI", and already run a practice-management system they will not replace.

## Product Purpose

**Spare Chair** (fictional) refills cancelled appointment slots from the clinic's own waitlist within minutes. When a slot opens, it messages the best-fit waiting patients in order, the first to confirm gets the slot, and the diary updates. Success for a visitor: understand that a cancellation stops being a lost hour, and start a trial.

## Positioning

Backfill, not reminders. Reminder tools try to stop the no-show; Spare Chair assumes some will happen and fills the gap. A neighbouring reminder product could not truthfully claim "the slot is full again before the patient has left the car park."

## Operating Context

A cancellation arrives by text, phone or a missed confirmation. The diary shows a hole of 30 to 90 minutes. The waitlist lives in a notebook, a spreadsheet or the practice system. Rules that matter: treatment length must fit the gap, some patients only want mornings, new patients and emergencies rank differently, and messages must respect consent.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Concept only. Illustrative capabilities: waitlist with fit rules (length, day, time, clinician), ordered offers by text/email, first-yes-wins claim, diary write-back, a log of every offer. Integrations shown are generic categories and invented connector names, not real partnerships. Pricing tiers, numbers and the demo are illustrative. The signup form sends nothing.

## Brand Commitments

Plain, dry, practical voice. No "AI-powered" language. No health claims. Must read as a fictional concept project everywhere it is presented.

## Evidence on Hand

None real. No customers, no metrics, no testimonials exist. All figures, quotes and clinic names on the site are invented and labelled "illustrative" or "fictional". Do not present any as genuine.

## Product Principles

1. Show the gap being filled, not a dashboard of abstractions.
2. Every number is labelled illustrative.
3. Speak to the front desk as much as the owner.
4. Say what it does not do (no diary replacement, no clinical data).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA target. Keyboard-operable demo, reduced-motion respected, no information by colour alone, readable on a phone at the front desk.
